# Sunset Relay — a 5v5 tactical FPS

A round-based tactical first-person shooter in three.js, built for make.fun's
["Make the best three.js tactical FPS"](https://make.fun/bounty/threejs-fps-02)
bounty ($1,000 in USDC).

**Setting:** a desert relay station at sunset — bright, readable, three lanes,
two bomb sites (A and B), crates, containers, solar panels and a relay dish.

**The game:** 5v5 rounds, first to 5 (max 9). Buy phase with an economy
($800 start, $300 per kill, $3,250 round win / $1,900 loss), five weapons,
three grenade types and kevlar. Plant the bomb at A or B (3 s channel),
defuse it (5 s), 35 s bomb timer with accelerating beeps. Bots push, hold,
plant, defuse and retake — and react to sight and sound. Headshots deal 2x
damage; recoil and movement spread punish firing on the move.

**Play it:** `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop
Chrome. WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, R reload, 1–5/Q/wheel weapons,
G grenade, F cycle grenade, hold E plant/defuse, B buy menu, Ctrl/C crouch,
Space jump, Esc pause.
